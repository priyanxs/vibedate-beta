Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

VibeDate is a free date-planning website. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use it.

What stays on your device

Your plan (city, budget, venue, dishes, gifts, names you type into the message writer, date and time), your colour theme, your choice to close the music player and, if you choose to add one, your own AI key are saved in your browser's local storage on your device only. We do not receive them. You can erase them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Third-party services

To work, the site loads content from other companies. They may receive your IP address and browser details, and may set their own cookies, under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense shows ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads, including personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player) plays the background music. You can close the player at any time.
  • Google Maps is loaded only when you press “Show map & reviews”. Map, directions and photo links open Google pages in a new tab.
  • Google Fonts supplies the text fonts.
  • Google Gemini is contacted only if you paste your own API key into the assistant's settings; your chat messages then go to Google. Without a key, the built-in assistant answers on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

Photos and information

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons contributors under free licences and are credited in the footer. Venue and menu details, prices and photos are illustrative; please confirm with the venue before you go. Links to shops or search results are for convenience and we are not responsible for third-party sites.

Children

VibeDate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data to remove everything saved on your device.
  • Use your browser's settings or an ad blocker to limit cookies and ads.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may be asked for consent for ads by Google's consent message.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
